Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported

Apple Vision Pro with visionOS 2.6 or later.

Is the app free

Yes. VivaMonarch is free to download and use.

Why does the app request camera or motion access

Only to enable spatial features such as rendering and interaction. You can manage permissions in Settings → Apps → VivaMonarch.

Do you store any personal data

No. We do not collect, store, or share personal information. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug. How can I report it

Please send an email with steps to reproduce and if possible a short screen recording.
